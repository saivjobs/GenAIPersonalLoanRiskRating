# Bureau Score and Employment Tenure Policy

## Bureau Score Bands
- 750 and above: excellent; a strong positive factor, largely offsetting
  minor concerns elsewhere in the profile.
- 690-749: good; supports Low-band placement absent other red flags.
- 630-689: fair; treat as a neutral-to-mild risk factor on its own. Do not
  independently justify a High-band score from bureau score alone in this
  range -- combine with DTI, liquidity, and document findings to determine
  final placement.
- Below 630: poor; a material risk factor. Combined with any delinquency or
  high utilization signal, this typically supports a High or Very High
  score.

## Employment Tenure
- 24+ months at current employer: treat as a stability factor that can
  modestly offset other moderate concerns.
- 12-24 months: neutral; do not treat as a risk factor on its own.
- Under 12 months: note as a mild risk factor, particularly when combined
  with income variability or a first-time borrower profile. Short tenure
  alone should not push a score above 6 unless paired with other material
  concerns.

## Thin-File and New-to-Credit Applicants
Applicants with sparse bureau history (thin file) should not be
automatically treated as high risk. Per fair-lending guidance, alternative
signals -- consistent banking behavior, verified employment, low DTI -- can
support a defensible Low-to-Moderate score even without extensive bureau
history. A thin file should be treated as a confidence caveat requiring
more document-based verification, not as an automatic risk penalty.
