# Risk Score Bands and Scrutiny Levels (Master Policy)

| Score | Risk Level | Typical Profile | Required Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | Very Low | Excellent credit history, high repayment capacity, low DTI, stable income | Straight-through processing; auto-approve within policy limits |
| 3-4 | Low | Good history with minor blemishes, healthy DTI | Automated approval with light system checks |
| 5-6 | Moderate | Mixed history: thin file, moderate DTI, recent inquiries, variable income | Manual review by a credit analyst; income/document verification |
| 7-8 | High | Recent delinquencies, high utilization, high DTI, short employment tenure | Senior underwriter review; consider collateral/guarantor, reduced amount, or repricing |
| 9-10 | Very High | Defaults, charge-offs, active collections, severe over-leverage, fraud signals | Decline, or exception-only approval with committee sign-off |

## Scoring Discipline
The numeric score should move in whole or half-step increments justified by
named factors, not jump multiple bands based on a single data point unless
that data point is itself severe (active collections, confirmed fraud
indicator, confirmed default). When the PD decile from the trained model and
the qualitative document findings disagree by more than two bands, the
rationale must explicitly explain the disagreement rather than silently
picking one.
